Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Hayatiyate Scheduler handles information when an authorized creator connects a social-media account.

Information processed

The service may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, selected privacy settings, videos, captions, scheduled publishing times, publishing status, and platform-generated post identifiers. It does not request social-media passwords.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized account, schedule requested content, publish that content, monitor publishing status, prevent duplicate posts, and troubleshoot delivery errors.

Service providers

Information may be processed by the connected social platform, GitHub Actions for scheduled execution, and Neon for private database and media storage. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sharing and sale

Hayatiyate does not sell personal information. Information is not shared for advertising or independent third-party marketing.

Retention and security

Credentials are stored as restricted secrets. Content and delivery records are retained only as needed to operate and maintain the scheduler, then may be deleted. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

Authorization can be revoked through the connected platform's security or app settings. A deletion request may be sent through the official Hayatiyate social-media account.

Children

The scheduler is an operator tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service or platform requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent through the official Hayatiyate social-media account.
